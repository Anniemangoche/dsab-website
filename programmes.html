Areas of focus

Where our work can make a difference

These seven proposed areas provide a framework for supporting people with Down syndrome, strengthening families and encouraging inclusive communities.

♡
01 / 07

Parent and family support

Families need information, encouragement and a welcoming environment where they can share experiences and learn from one another.

For whom
New parents, parents and caregivers.
Possible activities
Family meetings, welcome conversations, information sharing and peer-support groups.
✦
02 / 07

Early childhood development

Supporting young children as they develop communication, movement, social interaction and everyday life skills.

For whom
Babies and young children with their caregivers.
Possible activities
Play-based learning, caregiver guidance and practical activities that support development at home.
▤
03 / 07

Inclusive education

Encouraging schools to create learning environments where children with different abilities can participate and progress.

For whom
Learners, teachers, school leaders and families.
Possible activities
Teacher guidance, inclusive learning resources and discussions about classroom support.
◎
04 / 07

Community awareness

Promoting accurate information about Down syndrome and challenging stigma, discrimination and common misconceptions.

For whom
Families, community groups, churches, schools and the public.
Possible activities
Community discussions, awareness materials, educational talks and public information sessions.
↗
05 / 07

Life skills and youth development

Encouraging confidence, communication, independence and meaningful participation among young people with Down syndrome.

For whom
Young people and young adults with Down syndrome.
Possible activities
Practical learning in communication, daily living, social interaction and everyday decision-making.
▣
06 / 07

Teacher and caregiver training

Sharing practical knowledge that helps teachers and caregivers better understand and support individual learning and care needs.

For whom
Teachers, caregivers and relevant professionals.
Possible activities
Educational workshops, practical guidance and accessible learning materials.
⚖
07 / 07

Advocacy and inclusion

Promoting equal opportunities and encouraging institutions and communities to recognise the rights and contributions of people with Down syndrome.

For whom
Community leaders, organisations, service providers and decision-makers.
Possible activities
Awareness meetings, partnership discussions and advocacy for inclusive education, healthcare and work.
i

About these programme examples: This is a demonstration website. The seven areas above illustrate possible programme directions for DSAB. They should not be understood as confirmation that these activities are currently running or that registration is open.

DSAB can build relationships with organisations that share a commitment to dignity, participation, education and inclusion.
