Get to know us

About DSAB.

The Down Syndrome Association of Blantyre is a community organisation in Blantyre, Malawi, working with and for people with Down syndrome and their families.

We believe that every person deserves to be recognised for who they are, valued for their abilities and given opportunities to learn, play, work and belong.

Our foundation

What guides our work

Our purpose, vision and mission guide our commitment to families, individuals and communities.

01

Why We Exist

Many families in Malawi receive little information about Down syndrome. Myths and stigma can leave parents isolated, and children can be excluded from school and community life. DSAB exists to encourage understanding, support families and help change these experiences.

02

Our Vision

A Malawi in which people with Down syndrome are respected, included and able to reach their full potential, with equal opportunities to participate in society.

03

Our Mission

To raise understanding of Down syndrome, support families and caregivers, and promote inclusion in education and community life in Blantyre and beyond.

Understanding. Acceptance. Inclusion.

Every person deserves a chance to belong.

Down syndrome should never be a reason for someone to be overlooked, excluded or denied opportunities.

Our belief

A child with Down syndrome should be known by their name, loved for who they are and given the same chances as every other child to learn, play, grow and participate in community life. Their abilities, interests and dreams deserve to be recognised.

What we stand for

Our values

These principles shape how we relate to people, work with families and engage with our community.

01

Dignity

Every person is valued, spoken to respectfully and treated as an individual.

02

Inclusion

We work for belonging in classrooms, workplaces and neighbourhoods.

03

Family

Parents and caregivers know their children best. We listen first and support together.

04

Honesty

We share accurate, careful information and challenge myths about Down syndrome.

05

Partnership

We work with schools, health workers, faith groups and other organisations.

06

Participation

People with Down syndrome have a voice in decisions that affect their lives.

Our direction

What we aim to achieve

We want to see a community where families are supported and people with Down syndrome have opportunities to participate fully.

  • Families know where to find information and support.
  • Teachers feel prepared to welcome every learner.
  • Communities understand Down syndrome and reject stigma.
  • Young people and adults have chances to learn, work and take part.

Our community

Who is DSAB?

DSAB is made up of parents, caregivers, volunteers, educators, health workers and friends who share one goal: building a more inclusive society for people with Down syndrome.

We welcome people and organisations who believe in dignity, understanding, equal opportunities and inclusion.
