A back-to-school checklist for inclusive classrooms

Six quick checks teachers can make before term starts.

A good start to the term makes the difference for every learner. This sample checklist is a short guide for teachers.

Six checks

  • Have I spoken to each learner’s family?
  • Is the seating welcoming and close to support?
  • Do I have pictures or objects to show instructions?
  • Have I explained to the class that people learn differently?
  • Do I have a simple way to check understanding?
  • Have I planned how to celebrate effort?
