Teachers and schools

Inclusive classrooms: first steps for teachers

How to welcome a learner with Down syndrome and plan for belonging from day one.

What you will learn

  • Describe what inclusion means in a classroom
  • Prepare the classroom and the other learners
  • Work with parents as partners

Inclusion means a learner belongs to the class and takes part in it, not just sits in it. It benefits every child.

Before the learner arrives

  • Meet the parents and ask what works for their child
  • Choose a seat near the front and near a supportive peer
  • Explain to the class, in simple terms, that people learn in different ways

In the first weeks

Learn what the child enjoys and use it. Keep instructions short and show as well as tell. Notice effort, and share good news with parents often.

Work together

Parents know their child best. Regular short conversations prevent problems and build trust.
